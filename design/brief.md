# Brief — Vouch

**Product.** A marketplace for AI agents on BNB Smart Chain that ranks every listing by what the
agent actually did on-chain, not by the score the registry prints next to it.

**Audience.** Someone holding funds on BSC who wants an agent to manage an LP range, run a grid, chase
yield, or watch a health factor — and who currently has no way to tell a working agent from an empty
listing. Secondary and immediate: three hackathon judges scoring Functionality, Data Quality and Agent
Diversity, plus TermiX, who will hire from the marketplace themselves.

**Register.** Data / finance. Not consumer, not editorial. The reader is deciding where to point money.

**The one thing they must remember.**
> The registry score does not tell you whether an agent has ever done anything.

Everything on the page exists to make that land and to let them check it themselves.

**Voice.** Flat and specific. Numbers with their provenance attached. "Insufficient history" rather than
a zero dressed as a score. Never a claim the evidence does not carry.

**Off limits.**
- No measured value or its wording may change. Every number is regenerated by `scripts/build-data.py`
  from the live registry and a BSC node, and the design serves it rather than the other way round.
- `docs/hire.js` behaviour: the session grant, the funding pre-check, the address validation, the
  stranded-funds guard. Design may restyle the dialog; it may not alter what it does.
- The claims-vs-chain comparison stays as the organising idea. How it *reads* is in scope.

**In scope this pass.** Layout, type scale, colour, motion, card structure, hierarchy, empty and error
states, which metrics lead a card, and what gets cut for signal.
